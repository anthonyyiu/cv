Interaction Design Summit

Three-day conference focused on UX research methods and prototyping tools. Attended workshops on Figma advanced techniques and user testing frameworks.

TypeConference
Duration3 days
RoleAttendee
FocusUX Research & Prototyping

Overview

The Interaction Design Summit was a three-day intensive conference bringing together designers, researchers, and prototyping experts from around the world. The event focused on practical applications of UX research methods and modern prototyping tools.

I attended to deepen my understanding of user testing frameworks and advanced Figma techniques that could be applied to my personal projects and future client work.

Key Workshops

The summit featured hands-on workshops covering topics like rapid prototyping, user interview techniques, and design system implementation. I participated in sessions on advanced Figma components and auto-layout strategies.

One of the most valuable sessions covered user testing frameworks that I've since applied to my Gesture-Based Navigation Prototype, helping me structure more effective usability tests.

Networking & Takeaways

Beyond the workshops, the summit provided excellent networking opportunities with industry professionals. I connected with designers working on similar accessibility challenges and learned about emerging tools in the prototyping space.

The key takeaway was the importance of iterative testing early in the design process. This reinforced my approach to personal projects, where I now build quick prototypes to test assumptions before committing to full implementations.
