Research / User experience

UX Ethnography
(VRRA).

This was a course-long collaborative group project for IAT333 — Interaction Design Methods. We were tasked to find and work with a participant group to analyze their user experience and improve the adoption process.

RoleResearcher
FormatEthnographic study
MethodsObservation · synthesis
FocusHuman experience

Start with people

The main objective of this project was to identify pain points in the Vancouver Rabbit Rescue & Advocacy adoption process through ethnographic research and propose a design solution that addresses both adopter and coordinator needs.

Our team conducted interviews with the Adoption Coordinator, volunteers, and several adopters, both returning and new. We also hosted online workshops and analyzed existing materials to understand workflows and frustrations.

What I learned

One of the biggest challenges I faced was synthesizing qualitative data from multiple sources into clear themes. Through thematic analysis, we discovered that the existing forms were outdated, the process was lengthy, there were educational gaps around rabbit care, and there was tension between approving adopters quickly and ensuring they were qualified.

This project reinforced my interest in research-led design and helped me think more carefully about how to translate observations into a stronger user experience.
