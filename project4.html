Make it visible

Data can be technically correct and still feel distant. This project explores how visual hierarchy, pacing, and simple interaction can help a viewer move from overview to detail without losing the larger pattern.

I was interested in the moment when a collection of numbers becomes a story that people can actually follow.

A visual language

The work centers on clarity and rhythm: using shape, scale, and spacing to guide attention and create an experience that feels legible rather than overwhelming.

The result is a more personal way of encountering information, closer to exploration than to reading a report.
